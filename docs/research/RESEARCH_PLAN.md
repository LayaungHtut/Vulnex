# VULNEX Research Plan

Status: **PROPOSED** · Author: Claude · Date: 2026-11-06

Purpose: list what must be researched before major technical or commercial decisions are approved, who should do it, and which decision it unblocks.

**Done (desk research, TASK 002):** six-vendor competitive analysis, feature matrix, market opportunities and source register in this folder. These are documentary only: nothing was trialed, measured or validated with customers. I did not run any of that research and did not access any sources for this plan. Every item marked _Claude's current understanding_ below comes from my training knowledge, is UNVERIFIED, and must be checked against primary sources.

## Rules for all research

- Cite primary sources with retrieval dates. Mark vendor claims as claims (see the evidence classes in [RESEARCH_SOURCES.md](RESEARCH_SOURCES.md)).
- Write results to `docs/research/R<n>-<slug>.md` and update the matching ADR.
- An unanswered question stays **U (unresolved)**. It is never reported as a "no".
