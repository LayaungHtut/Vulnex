# VULNEX Execution Roadmap

Status: **PROPOSED** · Author: Claude · Date: 2026-11-06 · Approver: Founder

## How to read this

- The phases follow Master Instructions §20. Phases are **workstreams**, not strictly sequential gates. **Milestone M1** cuts a thin vertical slice through Phases 2–10 so that the whole scan-to-dashboard path is proven end-to-end before any phase is deepened. This deviates from strict phase order. The reason: building ten phases to full depth before anything runs end-to-end would hide integration flaws until late.
- **Effort assumptions:** the founder works about 20–30 hours/week with AI implementation help. Estimates are calendar weeks at that pace, given as wide ranges, with **low confidence**. They have not been calibrated against any completed VULNEX work. The first two phases will calibrate them, and the roadmap will be updated then.
- Nothing here implies enterprise readiness or Tenable parity. M1 is a reliable, evidence-backed, authorized scan-to-dashboard workflow on a **self-hosted reference deployment** (PRD Amendment A5, pending founder decision D2).
